## Getting started

Tomorrow needs two things to plan a morning: your calendar, and where you
start the day from.

1. **Connect your calendar** so Tomorrow can see your first commitment.
2. **Set your home address** in Profile, or let Tomorrow find it once.
3. **Allow alarms**, so the wake-up can actually ring.

Until the calendar is connected, Tomorrow shows an example day and says so.
Nothing on that screen will ring — it is there to show you what the app does,
not to wake you.

## Common questions

### My alarm did not ring

Work through these in order:

- **Is the alarm armed?** Open Alarms. If there is a black card asking to
  allow alarms, permission has not been granted yet.
- **Is your iPhone running iOS 26 or newer?** Tomorrow uses AlarmKit to ring
