## Getting started

Tomorrow needs two things to plan a morning: your calendar, and where you
start the day from.

1. **Connect your calendar** so Tomorrow can see your first commitment.
2. **Set your home address** in Profile, or let Tomorrow find it once.
3. **Allow alarms**, so the wake-up can actually ring.

Until the calendar is connected, Tomorrow shows an example day and says so.
Nothing on that screen will ring — it is there to show you what the app does,
not to wake you.

## Common questions

### My alarm did not ring

Work through these in order:

- **Is the alarm armed?** Open Alarms. If there is a black card asking to
  allow alarms, permission has not been granted yet.
- **Is your iPhone running iOS 26 or newer?** Tomorrow uses AlarmKit to ring
  through silent mode and Focus, and that needs iOS 26. On older versions the
  Alarms screen says so, and the times are calculated but not armed.
- **Is the alarm switched on?** A greyed-out row is switched off.
- **Did the event move?** Tomorrow rebuilds alarms when your calendar changes,
  so a meeting that shifted overnight shifts the alarm with it.

### Tomorrow is showing meetings I do not have

That is the example day. It appears when Tomorrow has not been given calendar
access, or when your calendar has nothing coming up. Connect a calendar from
the banner and it will be replaced by your own events.

### The wake-up time looks wrong

Tap the time to open **Why this time?** — it shows the whole calculation:
your first event, the arrival buffer, the travel time, and how long you said
your morning routine takes. If one of those numbers is wrong, Profile is where
you change it.

Travel time is estimated from the distance between your home address and the
event's location. If an event has no location, or a room name instead of an
address, Tomorrow falls back to a typical time for your travel mode and says
so.

### I want to skip one morning

Swipe the alarm and delete it. A calendar-derived alarm comes back if the
event is still there tomorrow, so deleting it dismisses it for that event
only. Switching it off instead keeps it visible but silent.

### I deleted an alarm I made myself

Hand-made alarms are deleted for good. Create a new one with **+** on the
Alarms screen.

## Subscriptions

Tomorrow Plus unlocks the whole week ahead instead of just the next morning,
and arms an alarm for every day your calendar starts early.

### Free trial

New subscribers get **3 days free**. You will not be charged during the trial,
and cancelling before it ends costs nothing.

### Managing or cancelling

Subscriptions are handled by Apple, not by Tomorrow:

**Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Tomorrow**

You can also open the same screen from Profile once you are subscribed.

### I paid before and reinstalled the app

Tap **Restore** at the top of the subscription screen. Your subscription is
tied to your Apple ID rather than to an account with us, so it comes back on
any device signed in to the same Apple ID.

If Restore finds nothing, check that you are signed in with the Apple ID you
originally bought with — a different Apple ID is a different subscription.

### Refunds

Apple handles refunds: **reportaproblem.apple.com**

## Privacy

Tomorrow has no servers and no accounts. Your calendar and location are read
on your device and stay there. The full policy is in `privacy-policy.md`
alongside this page.
